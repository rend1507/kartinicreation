About us

Kartini Creations makes custom knitwear and crochet pieces in Malang, Indonesia. Founder Putri Kapandeyan works each order by hand: sweater, beanie, accessory, or a full set.

Tell us the size, color, yarn, and pattern you want. We build the piece around that brief, with designs that sit between traditional craft and everyday wear.
